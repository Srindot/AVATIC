# output/: your submission

Put your submission here: see **[DELIVERABLES.md](../DELIVERABLES.md)**.

```bash
python3 output/collect.py
```

fills in your code, your best run, both executed notebooks, your
evaluation and the fair-play code check, and tells you what is still
missing. You add:

- `video.mp4`: the screen recording of your best run;
- `report.pdf`: your technical report.

Then zip this folder. It is never committed to git.
